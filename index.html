About Ingredients

When you are done researching ingredients in your petri dish, click "Record Findings" to download a video record to share with others.

🍄 Spore

Grows in a mycelium pattern with straight lines in cardinal directions. Continuously releases floating spore particles that drift and fade away. Reaches medium size and creates a misty atmospheric effect.

💎 Crystal

Forms beautiful fractal square spiral patterns inspired by bismuth crystals. Grows with sharp 90-degree angles in geometric formations. Each crystal has 1-6 branches that spiral outward creating unique snowflake-like structures.

🦠 Bacteria

Fast-growing blobby organism that moves like a lava lamp once it reaches sufficient size. Drifts smoothly around the dish with gentle pulsating movements. Creates organic, flowing patterns with pink and purple colors.

🌿 Algae

Grows in cellular compartments like biological tissue. Forms visible cell walls and boundaries between individual cells. Smoothly fades between neon green and deep blue colors, creating a bioluminescent effect.

✨ Sparkle

Grows in star-like patterns with a magical pulsating glow. Each sparkle blinks independently at its own rhythm creating a twinkling starfield effect. The glow has soft faded edges and cycles slowly.

🫧 Blob

Spreads in soft amorphous patterns while continuously cycling through rainbow colors. Features enhanced shimmer effects with frequent white sparkles. Creates a mesmerizing RGB fade that shifts smoothly through the spectrum.

🧫 Slime Mold

The fastest-growing organism that creates sprawling web-like networks. Older trails slowly fade away as new growth continues, creating a trailing effect. Reaches the largest maximum size and explores the dish in wandering patterns.

🪱 Nematode

A wiggling worm creature that continuously moves around the dish. Each nematode starts with a random color and smoothly shifts through the RGB spectrum as it moves. Wraps around the edges of the dish and creates mesmerizing rainbow trails.
